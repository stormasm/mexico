San Pedro Garza García features **varied topography that ranges from flat valley floors to very steep mountain slopes**, depending on the neighborhood or zone.

### **Key Geographic Zones**

* **The Valley / Flat Areas (Lowland SPGG):**  
  * Neighborhoods like **Del Valle**, **Centrito Valle**, and parts of **Santa Engracia** sit in the relatively flat valley floor.  
  * These areas are flat to gently sloping, making them the most walkable parts of the municipality with normal sidewalk infrastructure.  
* **The Foothills and Mountain Slopes (Hilly to Steep SPGG):**  
  * Neighborhoods built into the slopes of the **Cerro de Chipinque** and the **Sierra Madre Oriental**—such as **San Patricio**, **Chipinque**, **Olinalá**, and **Colinas de San Pedro**—are extremely hilly and steep.  
  * Elevations in San Pedro range from roughly 1,700 feet (520 meters) in the lower valley up to over 6,000 feet (1,800+ meters) as you go up the mountain.  
  * Streets in these upper residential zones often have sharp inclines, winding roads, and switchbacks. Walking long distances or commuting on foot in these hillside sections is generally impractical due to the steep grades.  
* **Northern & Eastern Borders:**  
  * To the north, the terrain is bounded by **Loma Larga** (a prominent ridge separating San Pedro from central Monterrey).

### **Summary**

If you are staying or navigating near **Centrito Valle or Calzada del Valle**, the terrain is largely **flat and easy to walk**. However, if you move south toward the base of **Chipinque**, it quickly becomes **decidedly hilly and mountainous**.
